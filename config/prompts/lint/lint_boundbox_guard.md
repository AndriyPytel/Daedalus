%s tests a guard against Shape.BoundBox instead of the geometry that carries the number. A BoundBox is read from the shape's triangulation, so on a curved or tangent face it lands inside the true extreme by the sagitta of whichever chord the mesh used, and a guard built on it fails against geometry the dimension was never wrong for. Take the number off the geometry instead -- a plane's Surface.Position, a cylinder's Surface.Center or Radius, a Vertex.Point -- and reserve BoundBox for the sign of a direction or which side of the solid you are on.
