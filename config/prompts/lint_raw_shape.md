%s came from PartDesign and a PartDesign feature computes its own Shape from its Profile — assigning it, or bolting fields on with addProperty, produces a feature tree that is fiction. Nothing raises: the sketch is decorative, Preflight recomputes clean, and the part stops rebuilding when a dimension changes. Use the feature that performs the operation — PartDesign::Pad, PartDesign::Pocket, PartDesign::Revolution, PartDesign::Groove, PartDesign::AdditiveHelix, PartDesign::Hole, PartDesign::Fillet, PartDesign::Chamfer — on a real sketch. If the shape genuinely belongs outside a Body, build it as Part::Feature and say so.
